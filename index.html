Score a goal, then solve the equation to make it count.

Controls

Player 1: W jump, A D move, S drop fast

Player 2: ↑ jump, ← → move, ↓ drop fast

On an iPad use the on-screen buttons. In 1 Player you can also use the arrow keys.

Rules

Knock the ball into your opponent's goal. Then jump into the bubble with the right answer to bank the point.

Get it wrong and your opponent can steal it. First to 5 points, or the most after 2 minutes. Win a match to pull for a new kit!
